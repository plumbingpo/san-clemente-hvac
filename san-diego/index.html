Keeping Your San Diego HVAC System Running Longer

A little routine care is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that breaks down in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

How old is your system? Its age tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and also shows higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement in peak season.

Small problems don't stay minor when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a miserable night.
